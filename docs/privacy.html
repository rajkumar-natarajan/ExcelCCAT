Privacy Policy

Excel CCAT - Canadian Cognitive Abilities Test Prep

Effective Date: January 22, 2026
Last Updated: January 22, 2026
🔒 Your Privacy Matters: Excel CCAT is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Excel CCAT ("the App"), developed by Curious Dev. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application designed to help students prepare for the Canadian Cognitive Abilities Test (CCAT).

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe learning environment for all users, including children.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Excel CCAT is designed to operate entirely offline and does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data or device identifiers
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • Browsing history or search queries
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health or fitness data
  • Any other personally identifiable information

3. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Practice Progress: Your quiz scores and progress are saved locally to track your improvement
  • Settings Preferences: Theme preferences (dark/light mode), sound settings, and notification preferences
  • Achievement Data: Badges and achievements earned during practice sessions

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data in your device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Excel CCAT is designed for students preparing for the CCAT, which includes children. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

Since we do not collect any personal information from any users, including children under 13 years of age, no parental consent is required to use the App.

5. Third-Party Services

Excel CCAT does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App operates entirely independently without connecting to external servers for data collection purposes.

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store, which has its own privacy policy governing app downloads and purchases.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features
  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • No cloud storage or backup of your data is performed by the App

7. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is stored locally and visible within the App
  • Deletion: Uninstall the App to delete all associated data
  • Portability: As data is stored locally, it remains on your device

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

If we make material changes to how we treat personal information, we will notify you through the App or by other means.

9. App Store Requirements

This App complies with Apple's App Store Guidelines and App Store Review Guidelines, including requirements for apps designed for children and apps that collect no user data.
